About Unfuck Your Taxes

Tax help should not make you feel small.

Sometimes a year gets away from you. Sometimes bad information piles up. Sometimes the whole system is designed to be hard to understand. We think getting help should be the easy part.

What we believe

Good tax work begins with the truth, not a sales pitch.

You deserve to know what is actually going on, what can wait, what cannot, and what a solution is likely to take. That means fewer scare tactics and more useful conversations.

  • Plain-English answers, even when the answer is complicated.
  • Priorities based on deadlines and real exposure — not drama.
  • We do not provide legal representation or legal advice. If a matter needs a lawyer, we say so and help you find one.
  • A focus on helping you stay current after the cleanup.

Who does the work

You will know exactly who is handling your case, and what they are licensed to do.

Preparation and cleanup work is handled by trained staff. Anything that requires representation before the IRS or a state taxing authority — collections, appeals, an offer in compromise — is handled or supervised by a licensed Enrolled Agent or CPA.

  • IRS representation is handled by a licensed Enrolled Agent or CPA with representation rights before the IRS.
  • You are told who is doing the work, and what they are licensed to do, before it begins.
  • All professionals are held to their governing standards of practice, including IRS Circular 230 where it applies.
  • No outcome is promised before your facts and records have actually been reviewed.

What makes us different

We start where you are. Not where you think you should have been.

No lecture about the unopened mail. No pretending a tax problem will disappear because someone says it can. Just a calm inventory of the facts and a workable way forward.
