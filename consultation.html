How fees actually work

We do not publish a flat number, because the work is not flat.

A single-year filing, a multi-year unfiled cleanup, and IRS collections representation are very different jobs. What stays the same is how you find out what it costs.

  • The initial conversation is free, with no obligation to move forward.
  • Fees are based on the actual scope of work, not a generic rate card.
  • You get the number in writing before any billable work begins — no surprise invoices.
