How it works

No rabbit holes.
Just a way through.

The exact work changes with your situation. The starting point does not: get the facts, take the heat down, and decide what comes next.

  1. 01 / START WITH THE FACTS

    Find out where things actually stand.

    Gather the IRS record, the filings, and the business or personal information that tells the real story. We replace assumptions with a clear snapshot.

  2. 02 / SORT THE URGENT

    Address what is expensive to ignore.

    Deadlines, collection activity, missing years, and notices all have different consequences. We make the order of operations visible.

  3. 03 / SCOPE THE WORK

    Know what is being fixed — and why.

    You should understand the job, the decisions ahead, and where a specialized professional is necessary before work begins.

  4. 04 / STAY CURRENT

    Build the routine that keeps the mess from coming back.

    Current books, good records, regular reviews, and timely filings are a lot cheaper than another cleanup.

A note on urgency

Already under pressure?

Do not wait for the perfect stack of paperwork before making contact. If there is a deadline, levy, garnishment, or notice, say so in your first message.
