01 / File my returns

Get your returns filed.

Current or prior-year returns with records ready to go.

Personal tax preparation

$250 per tax year

Your individual federal return and corresponding state return, where needed, using organized records.

Self-employed tax preparation

$499 per tax year

Your individual federal return, one Schedule C business, and a corresponding state return for freelancers and sole proprietors with organized income and expenses.

02 / Help with IRS penalties

Owe money to the IRS?

Send us the notice. We’ll look at the tax period and see if a relief request makes sense.

First-time penalty relief request

$399 per request

We check whether first-time relief fits, prepare and submit the request for one tax period, then follow up once and send you a status update.

Reasonable-cause penalty relief request

$749 per request

We review the facts, prepare and submit a documented request for one tax period, then follow up once.

Upload your notice before signing or paying. A practitioner reviews the facts and decides whether there is a basis to request relief. We can’t promise a penalty will be removed or that you’ll save money.

03 / Tax consultation

Need help with a tax problem?

Talk through a notice, catch-up filing, or a specific self-employed tax question with a tax professional.

Tax consultation

$98 one time

A focused 30-minute meeting, a brief review of the records we agree to look at, and written next steps.

Extended tax consultation

$249 one time

A 45-minute meeting, a deeper review of agreed documents, and written next steps. Available when your situation needs it.

Consultations don’t include tax prep, IRS representation, or a full multi-year investigation. After purchase, we’ll arrange a time with you.
