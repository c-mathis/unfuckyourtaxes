What we fix

Got a tax problem?
Bring it.

A notice. Missing returns. A balance you cannot pay. Books that are a disaster. It is usually more than one thing. We’ll help you figure out what to hit first.

01 / Tax relief

When the IRS is in your business.

Back taxes, notices, collection activity, and balances due are stressful enough. We start by figuring out what the IRS says is happening and what needs a response.

  • IRS transcript review and a clear status picture
  • Penalty, payment-plan, lien, levy, and garnishment questions
  • Priorities for urgent notices and collection activity
  • IRS representation by a licensed Enrolled Agent or CPA where a matter calls for it

02 / Tax prep

When you need to get filed.

Personal, sole-proprietor, and business returns are much easier when the numbers and records are not a guessing game.

  • Personal and business return preparation support
  • Information and records readiness
  • Amended-return and documentation questions
  • Tax-ready reporting and preparation coordination

03 / Unfiled returns

When you have years to catch up on.

Unfiled returns are not one problem. They are a series of years, records, and deadlines that need to be put back in the right order.

  • Catch-up filing plans for multiple years
  • Missing information and document inventory
  • Practical sequencing based on urgency
  • Records support for accurate, current filings

04 / Cleanup

When your books are a mess.

Cleanup gets your books, payroll, and records back to a place where they can support a return instead of making it harder.

  • Books and payroll cleanup
  • Catch-up bookkeeping before a recurring engagement
  • Monthly bookkeeping and tax-ready reporting
  • Quarterly estimated-tax habits and reminders
